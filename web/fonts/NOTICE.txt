Kouzan / Aoyagi brush fonts
===========================

These webfonts are derived from free fonts published by Musashi System
(武蔵システム), created from calligraphy by Aoyagi Kouzan (青柳衡山).

Included files
--------------
- kouzan-kaisho.woff2  — from 衡山毛筆フォント
  Source: https://opentype.jp/kouzanmouhitufont.htm
- kouzan-gyosho.woff2  — from 衡山毛筆フォント行書
  Source: https://opentype.jp/kouzangyousho.htm
- kouzan-sosho.woff2   — from 衡山毛筆フォント草書
  Source: https://opentype.jp/kouzansousho.htm
